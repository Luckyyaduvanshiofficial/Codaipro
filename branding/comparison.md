# Concept comparison — qualitative, NOT legal scores

| Criterion (out of 5) | A Offset Frame | B Isolated Kernel | C CP monogram |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Geometry discipline | 4.5 | 4.0 | 4.0 |
| Immediate readability at 24px | 4.5 | 3.5 | 3.5 |
| Monochrome consistency | 5.0 | 4.5 | 4.0 |

| Fit with warm editorial website | 4.5 | 3.5 | 4.0 |
| Perceived distinctiveness (subjective) | 3.5 | 3.0 | 3.5 |

**A:** Strongest silhouette, smallest internal detail budget; risk: general square-loop territory is crowded in software branding. One corner joint remains an optical adjustment candidate.

**B:** Encapsulated dark/light balance, clean single-path icon; risk: circular abstract marks with inset blocks are common and the inner negative shape is less meaningful without explanation.

**C:** Most explicit brand initials when enlarged, distinctive C/P relationship; risk: at 16–24px the P counter is delicate, and many software products use initial-based monograms. Its small-size rendering would benefit from optical variants after approval.

**Decision:** Recommend A for final refinement, not automatic acceptance. Check alternate corner configurations to distance it from existing corporate abstract square loops before production.
